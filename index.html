How it works. It listens for about 12 seconds, measures the gap between repeated sounds, then picks a musical tempo that divides evenly into that gap — so every beat lands with the machine. The microphone switches off before the music starts: otherwise the phone would hear its own music and follow that instead, and iPhones route sound to the earpiece whenever a mic is live.
